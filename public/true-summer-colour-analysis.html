What is True Summer?

True Summer is the purest expression of the Summer colour season. It sits at the heart of the Summer palette — cool undertoned, softly muted and gently blended. True Summers have a harmonious, understated quality to their colouring that responds best to colours with the same quiet elegance.

True Summers typically have cool pink or blue undertones in their skin, soft eye colours (grey-blue, cool grey-green, soft hazel), and hair that tends toward ash tones — ash brown, cool medium brown, dark blonde or soft black.

True Summer characteristics

  • Undertone: Cool — pink or blue hints beneath the skin
  • Depth: Light to medium — soft, blended overall colouring
  • Chroma: Muted — colours should be softened, never vivid
  • Contrast: Low to medium — features blend together softly
  • Overall quality: Cool, soft, delicate and harmonious

True Summer colour palette

True Summers look best in colours that share their cool softness — muted, blue-based and gently blended. Avoid anything warm, vivid or overpowering.

Best colours for True Summer

  • Powder blue and soft sky blue
  • Dusty rose and soft cool pink
  • Soft lavender and muted violet
  • Muted teal and soft blue-green
  • Dove grey and cool medium grey
  • Soft white and cool cream
  • Mauve and cool berry
  • Soft navy and muted denim blue

Colours True Summer should avoid

  • Warm oranges, terracotta and rust
  • Mustard, golden yellow and warm olive
  • Bright, vivid or neon colours
  • True black — opt for soft charcoal or navy instead
  • Warm browns and camel
  • Bright warm red — opt for cool berry instead

True Summer makeup guide

Foundation

True Summer skin has cool or neutral-cool undertones. Look for foundations described as pink-beige, cool ivory, cool sand or cool tan depending on your depth. Avoid golden, yellow or warm beige undertones — these make True Summer skin look muddy or sallow.

Blush

Soft cool pink, dusty rose, muted mauve and soft berry blushes are beautiful on True Summer. Apply lightly — your colouring is soft and blush should enhance, not overpower. Avoid peach, coral or warm pink.

Lips

Cool rose, dusty pink, soft berry, mauve and cool-toned nudes are your lip shades. A soft cool red works beautifully for evening. Avoid warm nudes (too yellow), warm coral or orange-based reds.

Eyes

Soft greys, muted mauves, cool taupe, dusty blue and soft plum eyeshadows suit True Summer eyes. Navy or soft charcoal liner enhances without overpowering. Avoid warm bronze, copper or golden tones.

True Summer hair colours

Best hair colours: Ash brown, cool medium brown, mushroom brown, dark ash blonde and blue-black all suit True Summer beautifully. Cool highlights in ash or silver add dimension without warmth.

Avoid: Golden highlights, warm auburn, caramel balayage, honey blonde or any warm copper tones — these fight against your cool undertone and can make your skin look sallow or tired.

True Summer jewellery

Metals: Silver, white gold, platinum and brushed silver are your metals. They echo the cool softness of your palette. Rose gold can work if it leans cool. Avoid yellow gold — it introduces warmth that clashes with your undertone.

Stones: Pearl, rose quartz, aquamarine, moonstone, soft amethyst, blue topaz and cool grey stones all complement True Summer's gentle colouring.
