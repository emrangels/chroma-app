Why do some colours suit you and others don't?

It comes down to harmony. When you wear a colour that matches your natural undertone, depth and chroma, it reflects light back onto your skin in a way that makes you look more alive, healthy and vibrant. When the colour clashes with your natural colouring, it can make you look tired, washed out or older.

This is why two people can wear the exact same outfit and look completely different. It's not about the cut, the brand or the trend — it's about whether the colour is working with their specific colouring or against it.

The four colour seasons

🌸 Spring

Warm, bright and clear. Best colours: coral, peach, warm turquoise, golden yellow, ivory.

💙 Summer

Cool, soft and muted. Best colours: dusty rose, powder blue, soft lavender, muted teal, dove grey.

🍂 Autumn

Warm, deep and earthy. Best colours: terracotta, olive, camel, burnt sienna, chocolate brown.

🖤 Winter

Cool, deep and high contrast. Best colours: true black, navy, ruby red, emerald, icy white.

The undertone test

A quick first step is checking your wrist veins in natural light:

  • Blue or purple veins → cool undertone. You likely suit Summer or Winter colours.
  • Green veins → warm undertone. You likely suit Spring or Autumn colours.
  • Blue-green veins → neutral undertone. You have more flexibility.

The fabric drape test

Hold a mustard yellow fabric near your face in natural light, then swap it for a dusty rose. The colour that makes your skin look alive, healthy and vibrant is a strong signal of your undertone family.
