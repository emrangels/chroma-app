Cookie Policy

Last updated: 9 May 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Emma Nagel (trading as Solla) uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit solla.com.au.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and provide a better experience. Some cookies are essential for the website to function, while others help us understand how you use our services.

2. How We Use Cookies

Solla uses cookies for the following purposes:

Type Purpose Provider
Essential Authentication and session management — keeps you logged in to your account Supabase
Essential Security — protects your account and prevents fraud Supabase
Analytics Usage analytics — helps us understand how the app is used so we can improve it Vercel
Functional Preferences — remembers your settings and preferences Solla

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use:

  • Supabase — authentication and database services
  • Vercel — hosting and performance analytics
  • Stripe — payment processing (only on checkout pages)

4. Local Storage

In addition to cookies, Solla uses your browser's local storage to save your session, colour analysis results, and preferences. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to third parties.

5. How to Control Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View cookies stored on your device
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all cookies

Please note that blocking essential cookies may prevent you from logging in or using core features of Solla.

For browser-specific instructions:

  • Chrome
  • Safari
  • Firefox
  • Edge

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
