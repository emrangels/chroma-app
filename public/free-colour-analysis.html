Why get a colour analysis?

Colour analysis solves one of the most common style frustrations — standing in front of a full wardrobe feeling like nothing works. When you know your colour season, you understand exactly which colours make you look alive and which ones work against your natural colouring.

The results are practical and immediate. Most people who discover their season describe it as a light-bulb moment — suddenly understanding why some outfits always worked and others never did, no matter how much they cost.

How is Solla different from other colour analysis tools?

Traditional colour analysis requires an in-person appointment with a trained consultant, often costing $150–$400. Solla uses AI to perform the same analysis from a selfie — free, instant and accessible from anywhere.

Unlike generic quizzes that ask you to describe your own colouring (which is notoriously inaccurate — most people misjudge their undertone), Solla analyses your actual photo. The AI reads your features directly, the same way a trained analyst would.

Tips for the most accurate free colour analysis

  • Use natural light — artificial light changes how your skin tone reads. Take your selfie near a window during the day.
  • No filters — even subtle filters can shift your undertone reading. Use your camera app without any enhancements.
  • Face forward — straight on is best. Angled photos can affect the accuracy of the analysis.
  • No heavy makeup — if possible, take your selfie with minimal or no makeup so your natural colouring shows clearly.
  • Neutral background — a plain wall behind you helps the AI focus on your features.
